# Alcácer do Sal domain (Sado basin) — assets and provenance

Static inputs for the Floodcast chain **coupled SUMMA–mizuRoute → FLUXOS** at **Alcácer do Sal**
(Sado river, Portugal). Hydrology runs in ONE executable: CH-Earth/summa `develop` with mizuRoute v3.1.1 built in
(`summa_sundials_mizuroute.exe -m fileManager.txt -c mizuroute_config.toml`); there is no separate mizuRoute run. Everything here is generated by [`build_domain.py`](build_domain.py)
and verified by [`check_domain.py`](check_domain.py) (`0 failure(s), 0 warning(s)` at delivery).
The files are plain files; the engine reads [`domain.toml`](domain.toml) and never imports the scripts.

> **Status: structurally complete, run end-to-end with the coupled image (see "Validation"), scientifically
> UNCALIBRATED and UNVALIDATED.** All SUMMA/mizuRoute parameters are *reasonable defaults* for a Mediterranean
> Atlantic-slope basin, chosen to make the operational workflow work; they are meant to be replaced by a proper
> calibration. Nothing here has been compared with a gauge. Treat any discharge or flood
> depth produced from it as a software demonstration, not a forecast.

## Provenance: real data vs. defaults

**Real open data**

| Quantity | Source |
| --- | --- |
| Terrain, basin, drainage, river network, FLUXOS DEM | Copernicus **GLO-30 DEM** (a *surface* model, DSM), 4 tiles N37/N38 × W009/W008, mosaicked, area-averaged to 100 m (UTM 29N) for delineation (pysheds D8, fill/resolve flats), bilinear to 20 m for FLUXOS |
| Land cover per HRU | ESA **WorldCover 2021 v200** (10 m, CC-BY 4.0), streamed as COG at ~30 m, class fractions per sub-catchment (`geometry/hru_attributes.csv`) |
| Soil texture per HRU | ISRIC **SoilGrids 2.0** sand and clay, 5–15 cm mean, HRU mean (all 29 HRUs succeeded; USDA triangle → ROSETTA class) |
| SUMMA tables and parameter files | copied from SUMMA `develop` revision `8d15bc36` (`utils/test/test_calibration/settings`, `containers/model-sources.toml`), then edited as listed under "Parameters" |
| mizuRoute `param.nml.default` | mizuRoute v3.1.1 (`28514dea`, the submodule SUMMA `develop` pins), channel `mann_n` edited |

**Rule-based defaults I chose (documented, not measured)**

- **Vegetation:** one USGS class per HRU from WorldCover fractions (tree ≥ 45 % → evergreen broadleaf 13;
  tree 20–45 % → savanna 10, representing open *montado*; else dominant of crop/grass/shrub). Result:
  23 savanna, 5 grassland, 1 evergreen broadleaf. Canopy top/bottom heights per class
  (`CANOPY_HEIGHTS` in `build_domain.py`) are assumed values.
- **Soil:** 25 HRUs sandy loam, 4 loam (ROSETTA indices 8 and 3), from basin-mean sand/clay. Depth
  fixed at 8 layers, 2.5 m total, for every HRU (no soil-depth data used; Alentejo soils are often shallower).
- **Parameters (reasonable defaults, `LOCAL_PARAMS`/`BASIN_PARAMS` in `build_domain.py`):**
  `aquiferBaseflowRate = 1e-8 m/s` at storage = `aquiferScaleFactor` (0.35 m), `aquiferBaseflowExp = 2` — about
  60 m³/s over the whole basin at that storage and a months-long recession after the wet season (the reference
  default 1e-7 would give ~600 m³/s); `k_macropore = 1e-3 m/s`; `rootingDepth = 2 m`; `tempCritRain = 274.16 K`;
  within-GRU gamma travel time `routingGammaShape = 2.5`, `routingGammaScale = 14400 s` (mean delay 10 h for
  GRUs up to 670 km²). Soil hydraulic properties come from the ROSETTA table class of each HRU, vegetation
  properties from the USGS table class. Everything else keeps the developers' reference values.
- **Decisions:** 34 explicit decisions (`modelDecisions.txt`): built-in backward-Euler solver (`num_method = homegrown`,
  no SUNDIALS needed at run time), `groundwatr = bigBuckt`, `infRateMax = GreenAmpt`, `nrgConserv = closedForm`,
  `aquiferIni = emptyStart` (cold start only), `subRouting = timeDlay`.
- **Cold state:** `coldState.nc` in the SUMMA `develop` restart format (dimensions gru, hru, dom = 1 upland domain,
  glac = 0): soil moisture half-way between ROSETTA θres and θsat (0.21–0.23), empty aquifer, 288.16 K, no snow.
  The first weeks of any run are spin-up; operationally the engine carries restarts (see ENGINE_NOTES.md).
- **Routing:** SUMMA routes within each GRU (`subRouting = timeDlay`), the **coupled mizuRoute** routes the GRU runoff
  through the 29-reach network with the **Eulerian kinematic wave** (`methods = "3"`, hourly step), Manning
  `mann_n = 0.035` (mizuRoute default 0.01 is a smooth concrete channel). Channel hydraulic geometry is computed by
  mizuRoute, not measured. Routed discharge `Q_reach(time, seg, method)` [m³ s⁻¹] is written into the SUMMA output file.
  **Limitation: the coupled build runs on ONE process (no `-g`, no MPI) and does not read/write a mizuRoute restart,
  so channel storage starts empty in every run** (see ENGINE_NOTES.md for how the engine handles that).
- **FLUXOS:** roughness height 0.15 m (≈ Manning n 0.035), no infiltration, no ADE transport, inflow at one cell.

**Not represented at all:** reservoirs and dams on the Sado and tributaries (Pego do Altar, Vale do Gaio,
Monte da Rocha, Roxo, Campilhas, …) and their releases — a major control on Sado floods; irrigation
abstractions and rice paddies; tide and estuary backwater at Alcácer; local rainfall and tributary inflow inside the
FLUXOS window (only the one main-stem hydrograph enters); buildings and levees; river bathymetry.

## Known limitations that matter operationally

1. **The channel is not resolved in FLUXOS.** GLO-30 is a DSM with roughly 2–4 m vertical error. Around the
   inflow point the valley floor is flat at 1.4–1.9 m (dem_info: 27 % of the window is below 5 m, 4 % of the cells are
   at or below 0.01 m, probably water flattened to 0 in GLO-30, and were floored to 0.01 m), so the Sado is a wide floodplain with no thalweg. Expect
   unrealistically large inundation extents until the planned bathymetry/topography survey (project
   `3_aquisition/…Levantamento_BUR_PC`) or a LiDAR DTM is burned in. The "town" point used for the outlet
   snapping is the castle hill (48 m); the river itself lies ~1 km away.
2. **FLUXOS inflow resolution is a correctness issue, not cosmetics.** While the domain is dry FLUXOS takes one
   time step as long as the interval to the next inflow row, so a coarse hydrograph is dumped into one cell at once.
   A 600-s test hydrograph gave **+12.7 % volume** (+152 000 m³ at every snapshot); the same hydrograph at 30 s
   conserved volume to **0.04 %** at 3 600 s (0.7 % at 900 s). `render_templates.write_fluxos_inflow` therefore
   resamples linearly to 30 s by default. Row *k* is applied over (t[k−1], t[k]].
3. **FLUXOS parser constraints** honoured by the build: ASCII `cellsize` must be an integer; DEM cells equal to 0 or
   NODATA become wall cells and negative elevations are replaced by their absolute value, so every DEM cell is
   ≥ 0.01 m; the inflow cell index is counted from the south row.
4. **Hydrology is uncalibrated and the coupled mizuRoute starts with empty channels.** See "Validation" below: the
   configuration runs cleanly and conserves mass, but nothing has been compared with a gauge, and in every run the river
   network starts empty (no mizuRoute restart in the coupled build), so the outlet needs ~5 days to reach the land
   baseflow level. The engine re-runs a few days of already-known forcing before T0 to absorb that transient.
5. **FLUXOS was run once** on this DEM/modset as a smoke test (1 h, 400 m³/s, 30 s inflow rows; ~2 s of compute): it loads,
   floods the valley floor from the inflow point and conserves volume as above. It used the Linux/aarch64
   `FLUXOS_cpp/bin/fluxos` binary (built from the local working copy, not verified to equal the pinned
   revision `c50cc215`) inside the local `fluxos:latest` image. The `SIM_DATETIME_START`, output folder and
   inflow paths must be container paths when run in Docker.
6. **HRU size is uneven** (9–668 km²): sub-catchments are the cells draining to each stream link above a
   150 km² initiation threshold. Change `STREAM_THRESHOLD_KM2` in `build_domain.py` to coarsen/refine.
7. **No reference gauge.** SNIRH/APA station codes for the Sado at/near Alcácer could not be verified from
   here and are deliberately not guessed: `[reference_gauges] stations = []`, `status = "unknown"`.

## Validation (coupled image, synthetic forcing)

`validate_coupled.py` runs the domain in the hydrology image built from SUMMA `develop`
(`summa_sundials_mizuroute.exe`, linux/amd64 emulated on an Apple-silicon laptop) with **synthetic** forcing: a
30-day spin-up from the cold state with ~4 mm/day of rain, then a 7-day run from that restart with a 3-day,
120 mm basin-mean bell-shaped storm (spatially varied, basin mean exactly 120 mm). Results of the delivery run:

| Check | Result |
| --- | --- |
| SUMMA + coupled mizuRoute finish, no warnings except two deprecated-parameter notes | yes |
| Wall time | 30-day spin-up 7 s, 7-day storm run 4 s, 6-hour chained step 2.7 s (emulated; includes container start-up) |
| Mass balance | SUMMA `balanceSoilMass`/`balanceAqMass`/`balanceVegMass` ≤ 4e-12 (numerically zero); outlet volume / basin runoff volume = 0.965 over the 7 days (the rest is still in channel storage) |
| Outlet reach 2029 (`upArea` 6 056 km²) | baseflow at the start of the storm run: land runoff ≈ 20 m³/s; hydrograph peaks at **798 m³/s** 63 h after the start (storm centre at 48 h); basin runoff sum peaks 5 h earlier at 837 m³/s; recession to ≈ 39 m³/s after 7 days |
| Runoff ratio of the storm | ≈ 14 % of the 120 mm (soils are deep, 2.5 m, and not saturated) |

These numbers only show that the configuration is sane in order of magnitude (mean annual flow of the Sado at Alcácer is
of the order of tens of m³/s; flood peaks of several hundred m³/s). They are **not** a skill assessment.

## Using it from the engine

See [`ENGINE_NOTES.md`](ENGINE_NOTES.md) for the exact command line, per-run directory layout, time conventions, output
variables and the restart/empty-channel behaviour. In short:

- **Forecast data:** `[geometry] hrus`, `hru_id_field = "hruId"`, `expected_hru_ids`, `geometry_version` and `[bbox]`
  plug straight into the existing `[remapping]` and `[forecast.bbox]` sections; SUMMA wants hourly forcing
  (`forcing_timestep_seconds = 3600`), interval-start time stamps.
- **Hydrology (one run):** `render_summa_file_manager(...)` + `render_mizuroute_config(mizuroute_path=...)`, then
  `summa_sundials_mizuroute.exe -m fileManager.txt -c mizuroute_config.toml -r e`. Discharge in m³ s⁻¹ is
  `Q_reach(time, seg, method)` of the SUMMA output file; the outlet is the `seg` equal to `domain.toml [mizuroute] outlet_seg_id` (2029).
- **FLUXOS:** `write_fluxos_inflow(path, times_s, discharge_m3s)` + `render_fluxos_modset(...)`; inflow location
  `domain.toml [fluxos] inflow_xy` (UTM 29N metres), also given in WGS84.
- The IFS 10 m wind height is encoded as `mHeight = 10`; SUMMA's 2 m screen-height variables are not tuned.
